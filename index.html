My First Time Cooking

Interactive Highlighter ✨

Click the structure buttons below to highlight and analyze the parts of the Recount Text!

Last Sunday morning, I decided to cook fried rice for my family because my mother was feeling a bit unwell. I woke up early at 6:00 AM and prepared all the ingredients in the kitchen.

First, I chopped some garlic, shallots, and red chili on the cutting board. Next, I poured cooking oil into the heated frying pan and cracked an egg into it. After that, I added a plate of cold rice and stirred it thoroughly with soy sauce and salt. The wonderful aroma filled the entire kitchen.

Finally, I served the special fried rice on a beautiful plate. My father and sister tasted it and gave me two thumbs up! I felt extremely happy and proud of my first cooking experience.
